About me

I like building things that are both technically solid and pleasant to use — because good engineering and good design aren't mutually exclusive.

I study Electronics, Telecommunications and Information Technology — which is a fancy way of saying I understand both why the hardware breaks and how to write the software that breaks it differently.

I gravitate toward the intersection of engineering rigour and creative implementation: from AI-powered cloud architectures to embedded systems and full-stack web platforms. I like things that work well, not just work.
